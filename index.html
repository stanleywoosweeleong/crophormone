Tropical fruit hormone charts

Buds lose gibberellin and gain cytokinin and ABA before flowering. Young fruit that drop show rising ABA and ethylene. At ripening, climacteric fruits peak in ethylene and non-climacteric fruits rely on rising ABA. Most crops have only the direction of change, not measured levels.

Ripening

Climacteric fruits (mango, durian, papaya, passion fruit, avocado) show an ethylene peak after harvest. Non-climacteric fruits such as rambutan stay low.
