# Ripening ethylene dominates a thin hormone record

Measured, chartable hormone values for tropical fruit trees exist mainly for **one hormone at one stage: ethylene production during postharvest ripening**. Mango, durian, papaya, passion fruit, rambutan and litchi fruitlets all have rates in µL C2H4 kg⁻¹ h⁻¹. For **flowering induction**, the accessible absolute concentrations come from just three sources: an LC-MS/MS leaf profile in mango (Shivashankara et al. 2019, ng g⁻¹ FM), an LC-ESI-MS shoot-tip profile in longan (Susawaengsup et al. 2011, ng g⁻¹ DW), and mango bud values from Upreti et al. (2013) that are known only through a review. For **fruit set and early fruit development**, the only absolute concentration found is citrus ovary GA1 (5.0 → 9.5 ng g⁻¹ DW at anthesis). Everything else is reported as fold-changes, percentage changes or timing. The qualitative pattern repeats across species. Before flowering, bud GA and IAA fall while cytokinins (especially zeatin/zeatin riboside) and ABA rise; paclobutrazol (PBZ), KNO3 and KClO3 amplify these shifts. During fruitlet drop, IAA falls and ABA and ethylene/ACC rise. During ripening, ABA peaks with or before the ethylene climacteric. The record is uneven, though. Durian, mangosteen, rambutan, guava, papaya, passion fruit, banana and avocado have essentially **no measured flowering or fruit-set hormone concentrations** in reachable sources. Two conflicts are central: longan GA during KClO3 induction goes up in the LC-MS study and down in the immunoassay and bioassay studies, and mango GA3 values differ by two to three orders of magnitude between methods. Only within-study series should be put on a shared axis.

This report draws on four research-note files, compiled from roughly 75 searches and page fetches. Many primary papers were paywalled or blocked: the ICAR repository was unreachable, and PMC sometimes showed a verification wall. Each value below therefore carries a **verification level**:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FT** | The value was read from the full text or a full-text page |
| **ABS** | The value was read from an abstract or abstract reproduction |
| **SNIP** | The value came from a search-result snippet; the original was not opened |
| **SEC** | The value was quoted second-hand by a review and not checked against the primary paper |

## Buds lose gibberellin and gain cytokinin before flowering

The most consistent signal comes from Indian mango work and from the Hohenheim–Chiang Mai litchi and longan programme. In mango cv. Totapuri and Alphonso, bud GA1, GA3, GA4 and GA7 fall over roughly the 30 days before bud break, while zeatin, zeatin riboside (ZR) and dihydrozeatin riboside rise ([Upreti et al. 2013, ICAR record](https://krishi.icar.gov.in/jspui/handle/123456789/17039?mode=full); [Burondkar et al. 2016](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40502-016-0236-1)). PBZ deepens the GA fall in shoots, by **−51.7% at bud break** in PBZ-treated relative to untreated Alphonso. It raises flowering-time ABA by **+59.9% in leaf and +41.1% in bud** ([ICAR record 5472](https://krishi.icar.gov.in/jspui/handle/123456789/5472?mode=full)). In Costa Rican Tommy Atkins, KNO3 raised bud Z/ZR "considerably" near induction, while PBZ lowered GAs in wood and bark ([Guevara et al. 2012](https://www.kerwa.ucr.ac.cr/items/cce740df-c903-4e3b-b4ed-b1dc2372bace/full)). Litchi under cool temperature and longan under KClO3 show the same direction: bud cytokinins up, GA and IAA down ([Hegele et al. 2010](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/863_40/); [Huang et al. 2021 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8215610/)). Pineapple forcing goes a different way, as expected for a monocot. Ethephon raised apex ethylene, ABA and 2-iP and lowered IAA, GA3 and zeatin, with an endogenous ethylene maximum near **day 8** after treatment ([Liu et al. 2011, AJOL listing](https://ajol.info/index.php/ajb/article/view/96078); [Li et al. 2016](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2016.00710/full)).

The absolute numbers behind this pattern are sparse and come in units that cannot be combined. In the one open LC-MS/MS dataset, mango leaf GA3 and GA4 sit at about 0.5–1.4 ng g⁻¹ FM and ABA near 100 ng g⁻¹ FM ([Shivashankara et al. 2019 PDF](https://bp.ueb.cas.cz/pdfs/bpl/2019/01/55.pdf)). The Upreti GA3 series, as quoted by the review, runs 395 → 137 (units presumed ng g⁻¹). That is two to three orders of magnitude higher, which points to older HPLC-UV quantification or a unit mismatch ([Cavalcante 2022 review](https://www.mango.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Mango-Flowering-Review_Italo-Cavalcante-atual.pdf)). The tables below keep each unit basis separate.

## Fruitlet drop tracks falling auxin and surging ABA and ethylene

Across mango, litchi, longan and citrus, abscission waves line up with low IAA and GA, and with rising ABA, ACC and ethylene. In mango cvs. Dashehari, Langra and Chausa, an ABA-like inhibitor stays high for the first **21 days** after flowering, when drop is heaviest. Seed GAs rise early and fall at the end of seed growth, and cytokinins peak twice ([Ram 1992](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/321_43/)). In longan 'Chuliang', GA4+7 peaks at **14, 44 and 92 days after full bloom (DAFB)** and dips at **29 and 60 DAFB**. IAA stays very low before 29 DAFB, and the troughs coincide with physiological drop ([Zhou et al., J SCAU](https://journal.scau.edu.cn/cn/article/id/199903107)). In litchi 'Wuye', control fruitlets at 35 days after anthesis (DAA) release **under 2 µL C2H4 kg⁻¹ h⁻¹**. Carbohydrate stress from girdling plus defoliation raises ethylene to about **15× control at day 2**, and abscission reaches **88% vs 28%** by day 7 ([Li et al. 2015](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpls.2015.00439/full)).

Citrus has the best mechanistic chain. In Satsuma 'Okitsu', defoliation-induced abscission is preceded by up to **20-fold** rises in fruitlet ABA and ACC, consistent with an ABA → ACC → ethylene sequence ([Gómez-Cadenas et al. 2000 record](https://buscador.una.edu.ni/Record/ReDivia5295)). In seeded sweet orange, GA1 in ovaries rises from **5.0 to 9.5 ng g⁻¹ DW** at anthesis, and emasculation causes 100% abscission ([Ben-Cheikh et al. 1997](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC158336)). In avocado, the seed is the main source of IAA and 2iP in 'Fuerte' ([ISHS 175_42](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/175_42/)), and the iP/ABA ratio rises linearly with fruit size in 'Hass' ([SAAGA 2000](https://www.avocadosource.com/Journals/SAAGA/SAAGA_2000/SAAGA_2000_PG_079-081.pdf)). Neither avocado source gave absolute concentrations.

## Climacteric peaks span two orders of magnitude, and ABA leads them

Ripening ethylene is the only stage where absolute rates can be compared across crops. One µL kg⁻¹ h⁻¹ equals one nL g⁻¹ h⁻¹. In Kenyan Tommy Atkins mango at 25 °C, peaks were **9.56 µL kg⁻¹ h⁻¹ on day 9** for less mature fruit and **13.29 on day 6** for more mature fruit. 1-MCP lowered and delayed the peak, down to 3.89 on day 21 ([Chomba et al. 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/horticulture/articles/10.3389/fhort.2025.1509989/full)). Durian 'Chanee' at 22 °C went from **under 0.02 to 8.5–10.4 µL kg⁻¹ h⁻¹** by day 8, a day after its respiratory peak ([Tongdee et al. 1989](https://rfcarchives.org.au/Next/Fruits/Durian/DurianRespiration9-89.htm)). 'Golden' papaya peaked at only **2.1 µL kg⁻¹ h⁻¹** (day 7, harvested green), and fruit picked at 16–50% yellow showed no climacteric rise ([Bron & Jacomino 2006](https://scielo.br/j/bjpp/a/NbrHVhtmcjpVgsVskW9W3Wv/?lang=en)). Rambutan is non-climacteric at **0.1–0.7 µL kg⁻¹ h⁻¹** ([UC Davis](https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/node/7981)).

Hormone sequencing is consistent where it has been measured. In Kensington Pride mango at 21 °C, pulp ABA rises at the climacteric onset (day 2) and peaks with ethylene on day 4, while IAA falls from its preclimacteric high ([Zaharah et al. 2012](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00344-011-9245-5)). Banana ABA likewise peaks on day 4, before or alongside ethylene ([Fruits/CIRAD](https://revues.cirad.fr/index.php/fruits/article/download/35873/34651/37916)). Litchi pericarp and aril ABA rises around **62 DAFB**, about a week before colouring and before a small ethylene peak ([Wang et al. 2001](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/558_42/); [ISHS 558_47](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/558_47/)). In the non-climacteric crops, ethylene drives ABA rather than following it: 35 µL L⁻¹ ethylene raised free ABA in Shamouti flavedo **3-fold at 12 h and 10-fold at 24 h** ([Brisker et al. 1976](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC542250)). In pineapple, deleting an ABA-responsive element from the AcoACS1 enhancer lowered both fruit ABA and ethylene ([Shu et al. 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2026.1769495/full)).

## Method, tissue and unit clashes break cross-study comparison

The biggest unresolved conflict concerns longan gibberellins under KClO3. The LC-ESI-MS study reports GA1, GA3, GA19 and GA20 *rising* in treated shoot tips and credits flowering to them ([Susawaengsup 2011](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/PL/pl/tech-docs/paper/101842)). The ELISA- and bioassay-era literature reports GA *falling* ([Huang 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8215610/); ['Fenglisui', Acta Hort Sinica 2015](https://www.ahs.ac.cn/CN/Y2015/V42/I2/332)). An air-layered 'Daw' study found no difference at all ([TCI-ThaiJo](https://li01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/joacmu/article/view/246243)). Tissue (shoot tip vs apical bud), timing (week 1 vs later) and method each differ, so this is not a clean contradiction. Still, the "GA down" consensus rests on the weaker methods. The same 'Fenglisui' study also found that bud zeatin was **not** positively correlated with flowering rate, which weakens the cytokinin-up model. Mango has a parallel problem with ethylene at induction. Florida Tommy Atkins showed no rise above basal ([Davenport & Núñez-Elisea 1991](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/291_11/)). Indian work reports 3–5× more ethylene in flowering shoots, and a Philippine thesis on KNO3-treated Carabao found an apex ethylene rise ([SEARCA abstract](https://www.searca.org/pubs/abstracts-theses-dissertations/database/view?absid=747)). In citrus, the field's own literature admits that a role for endogenous GA in flowering "has not ever been demonstrated"; the case rests on exogenous GA3 and inhibitor experiments ([UPV RiuNet](https://riunet.upv.es/handle/10251/33154?show=full)).

Unit and magnitude inconsistencies are just as consequential for charting. Mango GA3 is about 0.1–1.4 ng g⁻¹ FM by LC-MS/MS but in the hundreds in the review-quoted Upreti series. The Philippine shoot "gibberellin" value of **about 750 µg g⁻¹ falling to undetectable** is implausibly high for a true GA concentration and probably reflects bioassay GA-like activity. An auxin value quoted as "3.01 g g⁻¹ FW" is a garbled unit ([Cavalcante 2022](https://www.mango.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Mango-Flowering-Review_Italo-Cavalcante-atual.pdf)). Passion fruit peaks of **7.25 µL kg⁻¹ h⁻¹** (Brazilian yellow form, abstract) and **160–400 µL kg⁻¹ h⁻¹** (Hawaii guideline, snippet) disagree by 20–55×. Species form, maturity grouping or a units error could each explain the gap, and neither value has been confirmed in a primary table. Papaya behaves as climacteric in Australian work, with an ethylene peak about a day after the respiratory peak ([CSIRO EA9951173](https://publish.csiro.au/an/EA9951173)), but 'Golden' harvested past colour break showed no climacteric at all. Guava splits by cultivar into climacteric and non-climacteric types, so no single curve can represent the crop. In litchi, a seedless-cultivar snippet reports ACC *declining* at the main abscission wave, against the ethylene surge seen in seeded 'Wuye' ([PMC12604164](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12604164)). On citrus colour, pre-harvest ABA advances colour in Navel orange, but flavedo ABA in 'Valencia' rises without triggering colour break, and ABA-deficient 'Pinalate' still degreens normally with ethylene ([Sci. Rep. 2019](https://link.springer.com/10.1038/s41598-019-46365-8); [ISHS 463_8](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/463_8/)).

Several attribution problems also affect specific values. It is unclear whether the review's mango ABA values (85.7–106.3 and 52.3–65.5 ng g⁻¹) and the GA3 series belong to Upreti 2013 or Burondkar 2016. The girdled-row labels in the Shivashankara table are scrambled. The durian internal-ethylene values for 'Kan Yao' and 'Mon Tong' cannot be separated. The Chomba mango ethylene unit appears as mL in one version of the paper. The pineapple ABA snippet lists ABA as both rising and falling after initiation.

## Conclusion

The record is lopsided. Postharvest ethylene is easy to measure non-destructively by GC, so it is well quantified. Endogenous GA, cytokinin, ABA and IAA in field-grown buds and fruitlets need destructive sampling and mass spectrometry, so they are poorly quantified, and most of what exists predates modern LC-MS/MS. As a result, the textbook flowering model for tropical fruit trees (GA down, cytokinin up, ABA up) is supported mostly by direction-of-change data and inhibitor experiments. The one LC-MS dataset that tests it directly in longan contradicts it on GA. Any chart that places values from different studies on one axis will be dominated by method artefacts, not biology. The defensible comparisons are within-study series (Shivashankara, Susawaengsup, Chomba, Tongdee, Bron & Jacomino) and normalized relative-change plots.

The most valuable next steps are targeted, not broad. Retrieving full-text tables from Upreti 2013, Burondkar 2016, Chen 1991, Hegele 2010, Capelli 2021, Ma 2019 and the Shu 2026 supplement would roughly double the chartable absolute values. Digitising figures in Li et al. 2017 (lemon) and Olivares 2022 (avocado) would add more. Durian, mangosteen and rambutan need original LC-MS/MS work across the annual cycle; for these economically important Southeast Asian crops, no flowering or fruit-set hormone concentration has been published in any source this search could reach.

## Resolved conflicts

Two cells were marked Conflicting because studies disagreed. Both were checked against the primary literature:

- **Mango, flowering, ethylene: not a driver.** In Florida, ethylene stayed at basal levels in buds and leaves, and ethephon raised it without causing flowering (Davenport & Núñez-Elisea 1991). In the Philippines, blocking ethylene did not stop KNO3-induced flowering (Protacio 2003, 2009). Rises reported in India and the Philippines go along with developing flower buds, so they are a consequence rather than the cause.
- **Longan, flowering after KClO3, gibberellin: falls** (moderate confidence). Two labs using bioassay and immunoassay found GA down and cytokinin up in shoot tips and buds (Wangsin & Pankasemsuk 2005; Hegele et al. 2010; Huang et al. 2021 review). One LC-MS study (Susawaengsup 2011) found GAs rising in week 1, but its other hormones also disagree with every other lab.
